BEE Pictogram Show — Prop Catalog

Saturday Oct 24 · 3 actors + 1 prop runner · Grid squares = 10 cm · Red = dimensions

Finish: every prop is a flat silhouette painted solid matte white both sides — exactly like the pictograms (white on blue), and it pops against the actors' dark clothes on camera. No outlines, no detail, no other colours.
Materials: 4–5 large cardboard boxes · white matte spray paint ×3 cans (or white acrylic + roller) · box cutter + cutting mat · hot-glue gun · packing tape · 1 wooden dowel 60 cm · 1 white sheet (~150×200).
