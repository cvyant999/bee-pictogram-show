The Gospel of John
in 16 Pictograms

BEE Korea 청년부 Talent Show · Saturday, Oct 24

The idea

Tokyo 2020, but it's Jesus

Remember the Tokyo Olympics opening ceremony: performers in blue & white acted out all 50 sport pictograms in about 5 minutes. It was hilarious and brilliant.

We do the same with the Gospel of John — 7 signs, 7 "I am" sayings, the cross, and "Feed my sheep." Funny, physical, awkward… and pointing to Jesus the whole way.

The rule

Funny people. Holy Jesus.

✝ Jesus

  • Always the center figure
  • Calm, upright, still — never the joke
  • One person plays Jesus every scene

😂 Everyone else

  • Two side actors take the comedy
  • Exaggerated, awkward, hard poses
  • Scene 15 (the cross) is the one serious beat
How it looks

Live on screen, every 15 seconds

Pictogram pops up big
→
Slides left; live camera on the right
→
We get into the pose · 15s countdown
→
📸 Shutter, freeze, ✓ match!

A laptop in the front row films us; the projector shows the pictogram and the live camera side by side, so the audience can compare.
